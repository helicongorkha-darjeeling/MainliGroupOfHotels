# Mainali Hospitality booking operations

The hosted database publishes 25 rooms: 14 Double, 4 Triple, 6 Four-person and 1 Six-person. The website searches actual nightly capacity. Verified signed-in guests can reserve a category, see their bookings and cancel unpaid holds. Tentative holds expire after 24 hours; expired holds stop consuming availability immediately, with stored statuses swept every five minutes.

The public checkout starts with the room and price review. Pay now starts Google sign-in for signed-out guests and returns them to their selected stay. Google supplies name and email, not a contact phone. Guests enter their phone and explicitly continue to save `booking_contact_phone` in their Supabase Auth user metadata; it is not OTP verification and is never used for authorization. The authenticated `/api/guest-contact` endpoint works without payment secrets and only updates the caller's own contact metadata. Payment reservations then store these details in `hotel_reservations`, and secure Razorpay checkout receives the server-owned reservation contact prefill. Existing tentative bookings remain supported; new public bookings use payment confirmation. Counts above three are displayed as “Rooms available”; exact low counts and sold-out status always come from inventory, never repeat visits.

Nightly party prices are ₹2,000 for one guest, ₹2,500 for two and ₹1,200 per guest for parties of three or more. Capacity determines the number of rooms required. Manager-created seasonal rates apply per night; a lower seasonal quote can show the actual standard price as its reference. No seasonal discounts have been invented.

Taxes are unapproved, rather than assumed to be zero. Tentative quotes clearly disclose this. Online payment remains disabled until approved tax and policies are saved and payment credentials are configured. This setup does not determine GST registration or legal tax liability.

## Front desk access

Open [Staff login](https://mainli-group-of-hotels.vercel.app/staff/login) or [Admin login](https://mainli-group-of-hotels.vercel.app/admin/login) with a staff sign-in name and password. `/staff` routes managers/reception to `/staff/operations` and housekeeping to `/staff/housekeeping`; `/admin` remains a front desk alias. `Admin` maps to the existing owner manager account, whose password was already saved and tested by the owner. Managers may change their own password at `/admin/security` after password sign-in. Staff private requests require a server-verified password authentication claim and an active database role; a Google-only session cannot open them. Google remains available for public guest bookings. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth and never stored in this repository or user metadata.

The dashboard reads all active stays, future reservations, unresolved payment reviews and valid holds, plus the latest 500 historical reservations and all 25 rooms from the authenticated Supabase Edge gateway. It refreshes every 30 seconds when visible, pauses during saves, and provides manual refresh, guest/reference/contact search and status filters. Changes refresh immediately after Supabase confirms the save. Setting housekeeping to `out_of_service` removes that room from bookable capacity; the database rejects changes that would conflict with existing bookings.

The operations screen provides upcoming guest call contacts, tentative confirmation, unpaid cancellation, physical room assignment, check-in/out and housekeeping. Managers can set seasonal prices and approved tax, check-in/out times and cancellation terms. Confirmation through the front desk records a confirmed reservation without inventing a captured online payment. Paid cancellation/refunds require manual provider handling; late captured payments are flagged for review instead of reclaiming sold inventory.

**Adding staff:** sign in as Admin, open **Staff** (`/staff/team`), tap **Add staff**, enter the staff member's full name, unique sign-in name, role and a password you choose. Give each person their sign-in name and password privately. The same screen resets passwords and deactivates access. Creation/reset uses Supabase Auth admin inside the Edge gateway; no service key reaches the browser. Staff use individual accounts without Google. Active database roles are checked on every private request. Only managers manage staff, settings, discounts, check-in corrections or balance-due approvals. Internally generated Auth email identifiers are not deliverable addresses.

**Front desk:** home has Arrivals, In-house, Departures, Rooms with dirty count, and New walk-in. Rooms show cleaning/occupancy, booking source, server check-in time and expected departure when assigned. Check-out sets the room **Dirty** in the same transaction. When a key returns, reception selects the active housekeeper and taps **Key received, room clean**. The audit retains room, old/new status, front-desk actor, housekeeper name and server time. Dirty-to-clean requires this attribution. Legacy Unknown rooms can be inspected and marked clean as initial setup.

**Housekeeping:** `/staff/housekeeping` shows only the 25 room numbers/categories, cleaning status and Vacant/Occupied/Departing today/Arriving today. Dirty rooms come first, with Dirty/All filters, Refresh and visible-page refresh every 30 seconds. No guest names, contacts, prices, payments or private IDs are sent to this role. Writes are denied by Next.js, the gateway and SQL.

**Walk-in and check-in:** the wizard collects dates/party, an available room, guest details and optional advance. Shared category and physical capacity, saved prices and advances are checked transactionally. Non-cash advances require a reference. Save creates the reservation/advance together; check-in is a separate explicit action. A walk-in does not become a booking belonging to the staff member's personal guest account. Identical request retries recover the original reservation; reusing the key with changed details returns a conflict.

**Room registration:** tap a clean vacant room in **Rooms**, enter dates, guest profile, typed ID details, booking source and optional received advance, then tap **Check in** once. The reservation, assignment, private guest profile and server check-in time save in one transaction. Retries cannot duplicate the stay or advance. Open an occupied room to see its guest, arrival time, source, expected checkout and bill. Existing confirmed bookings retain their assigned-room check-in form.

Registration collects source/OTA reference, name, phone, optional email, address, nationality, adults/children and typed ID type/number. No photo upload is requested or accepted. Private `hotel_guest_profiles` records are linked to the stay; profiles are not automatically merged across guests. Only manager/reception can read them through the gateway; Aadhaar is masked to its last four digits. Legacy stored photos retain private read protection. Foreign guest fields include passport and visa details. The server sets check-in/out times and actors. Manager corrections need a reason and audit entry; reception cannot submit a custom timestamp. Expected checkout changes re-check physical and category capacity, including online holds.

Foreign arrivals/departures show an FRRO reminder; submission is performed separately by hotel staff. Rule 17 of the [Immigration and Foreigners Rules 2025](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Immigration_and_Foreigners_Rules_2025_16092025.pdf) specifies reporting within 24 hours using Form III (formerly Form C). Use the [official portal](https://www.indianfrro.gov.in/frro/FormC/login.jsp) and the hotel's applicable procedure.

**Bill and payment:** folios use saved nightly prices, extras, manager discounts, taxable value, approved tax and receipts in integer paise. The approved tax rate is retained once financial entries begin; issued invoice charges and dates are immutable. Take payment supports cash, card on the hotel terminal and manual hotel UPI. Non-cash receipts require a reference; every receipt records its staff actor. Retry keys prevent duplicate charges, discounts and receipts.

Razorpay test Payment Links and single-use UPI QR require test keys and the webhook events below. Only a verified, provider-checked captured payment enters the folio. Late/duplicate captures remain visible for reconciliation; overpayments show credit/refund review. Refunds are handled separately at the provider. Do not collect real money using test links or QR. Checkout requires a zero balance or explicit manager **Allow balance due** approval with a reason; reception cannot override it.

**Food and checkout invoice:** open the occupied room and tap **Add food bill** for a description and amount. This joins room nights and other extras in the stay's bill. After settlement (or manager balance approval), **Check out & create invoice** creates the numbered Mainali invoice, records departure and marks the room dirty in one transaction. If invoice settings or any check fail, the guest remains checked in; retrying cannot issue another invoice. The invoice opens automatically with the Mainali logo, guest/address, room/stay, food and other line items, taxable value, tax, total, paid and balance.

Hotel legal name/address and approved tax settings are required; GSTIN must match the owner-approved treatment. Sequential numbers are assigned per Indian financial year, e.g. `MH/2026-27/0001`. Later receipts update only settlement amounts. Share creates an expiring revocable opaque link exposing the bill but no IDs, passport/visa, contacts or staff identifiers. WhatsApp opens a draft for staff to send; email uses Resend if configured. A new share rotates the old link. Anyone with a valid link can read the bill, so share only with the guest; Revoke link disables it. Shared pages are uncached and excluded from indexing.

## Secure provider configuration

Configure these in the **Supabase project `yefndxkljepxhoeclknl` → Edge Functions → Secrets**, never in Git or chat:

- `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`, `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` — start with test credentials. `RAZORPAY_MODE` defaults to test; use `live` only after a real test checkout and launch approval.
- `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL` — use the verified sender you approve.
- `HOTEL_CRON_SECRET` — a random secret of at least 32 characters.

Store the same scheduler secret in Supabase Vault under the name `hotel_cron_secret`. The existing five-minute reminder schedule makes no outbound request until that Vault secret exists. Both the Edge secret and Vault secret are required. Reminders use Resend and stable idempotency keys; the front-desk call list is available independently. No automated phone calls are configured.

Configure Razorpay captured-payment webhook events (`payment.captured`, `order.paid`, `payment_link.paid` and `qr_code.credited`) to `https://mainli-group-of-hotels.vercel.app/api/payments/webhook`, with the matching webhook secret. Orders and captured amounts are checked server-side. A payment attempt receives a 15-minute hold. Confirmation requires a verified captured payment; callbacks alone never confirm a booking.

In Razorpay, start in Test mode and generate the Key ID and Key Secret under Account & Settings → API Keys. Add the webhook above with a separate secret and all four listed events. Confirm automatic payment capture and the desired payment methods. Store these three values in Supabase Edge Functions → Secrets as listed above, with `RAZORPAY_MODE=test`. These secrets belong to the Edge booking gateway; adding them only in Vercel does not configure it. Complete a test checkout and webhook replay before real payments. For Live mode, complete Razorpay account activation/KYC and settlement bank setup, submit the production website for verification, then generate Live keys and set `RAZORPAY_MODE=live`. Hotel-approved taxes and final booking/cancellation policies must also be saved in Front desk operations before payments are enabled.

Official setup references: [API keys](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/account-settings/api-keys/), [webhooks](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/setup-edit-payments/), [capture settings](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payments/capture-settings), [Standard Checkout](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/web-integration/standard/integration-steps/).

Checkout lists UPI first and retains the other methods enabled on the Razorpay account. It hides UPI Collect and uses Razorpay's device-specific Intent/QR flow. On Android and iOS mobile web, guests choose a supported app such as GPay or PhonePe; the app opens with the payment details, and the guest approves with their UPI PIN. Desktop checkout displays a QR to scan with a phone. UPI Intent must be enabled on the merchant account (usually enabled by default; contact Razorpay support if missing). A separate personal GPay QR or UPI ID is not required for this gateway integration. Booking confirmation still requires the server-verified captured payment for the reservation order. Test app switching and return on real Android/iOS phones, and desktop QR payment, before enabling live bookings. Test-mode simulation alone does not verify real app switching. References: [UPI Intent and platform support](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/upi/upi-intent), [Intent/QR checkout configuration](https://razorpay.com/docs/announcements/upi-collect-migration/standard-integration).

Next.js needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and the appropriate `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in local/Vercel configuration. Booking mutations use the authenticated Edge gateway; its service-role credential remains inside Supabase. Legacy `BOOKING_MODE`, local Razorpay credentials and the old expiry route do not control the new category reservation system.

## Google sign-in URLs

In hosted Supabase **Authentication → URL Configuration**, the Site URL must be `https://mainli-group-of-hotels.vercel.app`. Add the exact allowed callback `https://mainli-group-of-hotels.vercel.app/auth/callback`. Optional development callbacks are `http://localhost:3000/auth/callback` and `http://127.0.0.1:3000/auth/callback`. The hosted Auth configuration is separate from `supabase/config.toml`, which configures a local Supabase stack.

Google's Authorized redirect URI remains `https://yefndxkljepxhoeclknl.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`; Google returns to Supabase first. The website requests a fixed callback without booking query parameters, stores the validated stay destination in a ten-minute cookie and deletes it on return. Start sign-in from the production website in the same browser that will receive the callback. A localhost PKCE cookie cannot authenticate a different production host. After changing settings, restart sign-in instead of reusing an old Google or callback URL.

## Verification before enabling paid bookings

1. Test Google sign-in on localhost and production with the correct Supabase redirect allowlist.
2. Create a tentative reservation; check its booking history, front-desk entry, inventory reduction and cancellation/release.
3. Approve taxes and policies, then enable payments in manager settings with test keys configured. Complete a captured payment and replay its webhook; confirm a single booking/payment result.
4. Test an expired or cancelled hold followed by a late capture; check the payment-review workflow.
5. Configure Resend and both scheduler secrets. Verify an actual receipt and reminder, including job retry state.
6. Switch to live provider credentials only after these checks and final hotel policy approval.
